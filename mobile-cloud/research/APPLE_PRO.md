# Apple Pro presentation

New materials layer: deep black, restrained blue illumination, rounded glass surfaces, capsule navigation and tabular price typography. The existing chart, five assets, providers, strategies, levels and confidence gates are preserved.

The standalone presentation module performs no network calls or price interpolation. It animates page opacity/position, actual received quote highlights, selection pills and an atmospheric background. A 24-animation cap bounds transient animation resources. Motion pauses while hidden; pointer glow is restricted to fine pointing devices and updated through animation frames.

System reduced-motion preferences are respected by default. The app toggle permits an explicit opt-in, stores that choice on the device and supports turning motion off. The mobile header does not establish a filtered containing block for its fixed bottom navigation.

Verified in the actual browser: on/off control, running background keyframe, pane switching, fixed bottom navigation and no horizontal page overflow at 390 × 844. No generated raster/video or paid Higgsfield job is needed for these effects.
